Welcome to Click-In-Clinic

Our platform empowers patients to take control of their health by providing a convenient way to assess symptoms. Patients can complete an assessment, which is then securely transmitted to their doctor/healthcare-team. Healthcare professionals utilize the app to review patient assessments, ask follow-up questions, and make accurate diagnoses. With Click-In-Clinic, quality healthcare is just a click away!

Provides suggested tests and potential diagnoses to Healthcare Professionals

The application provides suggested questions based on the questions anwered in the assessment. Additionally, on completion of an assessment, Healthcare Professionals can see recommended tests and potential diagnoses for the completed assessment.

We Prioritize Security

Patient information can only be accessed by verified healthcare professionals. Admins are responsible for verifying healthcare professionals that sign-up. Additonally, Institution admins can be assigned and given admin privlages to verify healthcare professionals in their institiutions.
